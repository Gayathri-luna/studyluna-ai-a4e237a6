# StudyLuna.ai intelligence and naming update

## Goal
Make the assistant feel natural, intelligent, concise, and easy to understand, while consistently presenting it to students as **StudyLuna.ai**.

## Changes
1. **Adaptive answer intelligence**
   - Add the final intelligence rule to the assistant instructions: understand intent, choose the best approach and detail level, prioritize accuracy, format for clarity, and remove unnecessary content.
   - Replace the rigid default “concept → example → step-by-step → quick revision” pattern with an adaptive rule.
   - Use a direct answer first; add concepts, examples, formulas, steps, or revision points only when they genuinely help.
   - Keep educational solutions complete, but avoid repeated explanations, duplicate conclusions, filler, and unnecessary headings.
   - Preserve multilingual behavior, including natural Telugu-English responses.

2. **Clear, readable responses**
   - Keep short paragraphs and useful spacing without forcing every sentence into a separate section.
   - Use bullets, numbered steps, tables, and academic stages only when suitable for the question.
   - Keep simple questions brief and make difficult questions understandable without becoming clumsy.
   - Retain the existing accuracy, privacy, attachment, YouTube, and tool-honesty rules.

3. **Rebrand Luna AI as StudyLuna.ai**
   - Replace user-visible “Luna AI”, “LunaAI”, and versioned chat labels with **StudyLuna.ai** across the chat, home search, navigation, dashboard, footer, supporting pages, notices, errors, and page metadata.
   - Rename visible model choices consistently under the StudyLuna.ai brand while preserving their current capabilities and selection behavior.
   - Update the premium page wording to **StudyLuna.ai V3 Pro** while keeping its ₹299/month offer and payment-ready behavior unchanged.
   - Preserve existing URLs, saved chats, internal storage keys, and code identifiers so the rebrand does not break functionality.

4. **Verification**
   - Search for remaining old user-visible branding and remove unintended duplicates.
   - Test a simple question, a detailed academic question, and a Telugu-English question to confirm answers are clear, adaptive, and non-repetitive.
   - Verify chat, model selection, creation tools, uploads, navigation, and premium page still work in light and dark themes.
